Assalamu Alaikum Zahid,

Your digital boutique is now technically complete and ready for you to inhabit. This document explains how you will manage your brand, products, and orders.

### 1. PRODUCT MANAGEMENT (Sanity Studio)
**URL:** zahidaan.sanity.studio

This is where you "fill your shelves."
- **Adding Products:** You can add new Attars, Ouds, or Perfumes.
- **Managing Stock:** Update prices or stock levels here.
- **Bestsellers:** Mark any product as a "Bestseller" to highlight it on the homepage.
- **Images:** Upload your editorial product photography directly here.

*Note: After you click "Publish" in Sanity, the website will automatically rebuild and show the new content within ~5 minutes.*

---

### 2. ORDER MANAGEMENT
When a customer places an order:
1. **WhatsApp Notification:** You will receive a pre-filled message on your WhatsApp with the customer's name, address, and order total.
2. **Confirmation Email:** You will receive an email from `orders@zahidaan.in`.
    - **Verify Payment:** For UPI orders, check your PhonePe Business app first.
    - **One-Click Confirm:** Click the "Confirm Order" button inside the email. This notifies the customer that their order is being prepared.

---

### 3. PAYMENTS & SHIPPING
- **Payments:** All UPI payments go directly to your linked bank account via the PhonePe Business QR code. There are zero transaction fees (0% MDR).
- **Shipping (Local):** For Patancheru/Isnapur orders, you or your runner deliver the item and collect cash (if COD).
- **Shipping (Pan-India):** Use your Shiprocket dashboard to generate labels and schedule pickups for orders outside your local area.
