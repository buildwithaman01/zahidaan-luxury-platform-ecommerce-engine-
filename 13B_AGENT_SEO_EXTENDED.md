## SECTION 8: LINK BUILDING STRATEGY

Agent 13 covers on-page SEO, technical SEO, and schema in detail. What it does not cover is **how ZAHIDAAN earns inbound links** — the single biggest off-page ranking factor for Google. A new domain with zero backlinks will not rank on page 1 for competitive terms no matter how perfect the on-page work is.

### 8.1 Why ZAHIDAAN Needs Links

A fresh `.in` domain starts with zero domain authority. The Cluster A keywords ("attar shop in Patancheru", "best attar hyderabad") have low competition — a handful of quality links will push ZAHIDAAN to page 1. Cluster B keywords ("buy attar online india", "arabian oud perfume online india") have national competition from Ajmal, Rasasi, and niche aggregators — links from relevant sites are non-negotiable to compete.

Target: **20 quality referring domains in the first 6 months.**

### 8.2 Link Acquisition Tactics

#### Tactic 1 — Local Business Citations (Already in Agent 13)
JustDial, Sulekha, IndiaMart etc. from Section 3.5. These are low-DA but locally relevant. Do all 10 on launch day.

#### Tactic 2 — Fragrance Blogger Outreach

Target: Hindi, Telugu, and English fragrance/lifestyle bloggers with at least 5,000 monthly readers.

**Outreach process:**
1. Search Google for: `"attar review" site:blogspot.com OR site:wordpress.com`, `"perfume blog india"`, `"ittar review hindi"`
2. Find 10 bloggers who have reviewed oud or attar in the last 12 months
3. Send them a sample + a WhatsApp or email pitch (template below)
4. Ask for: a product review post with a link to `zahidaan.in/product/[slug]`

**Blogger outreach template (WhatsApp/Email):**
```
Assalamualaikum [Name],

I came across your review of [post name] — beautiful writeup, you clearly understand attar.

We just launched ZAHIDAAN Attars & Perfumes in Patancheru (Hyderabad) — a boutique brand 
focused on authentic, alcohol-free attars and Arabian ouds. We'd love to send you a sample 
of our [product name] — no obligation, just share your honest opinion if you love it.

Would that work for you?

— [Your name], ZAHIDAAN Attars & Perfumes
zahidaan.in | [phone]
```

Expected outcome: 2–4 review posts from 10 outreach attempts. Each earns 1 dofollow link.

#### Tactic 3 — Reddit and Forum Presence

**Target communities:**
- `r/indiafragrance` (active, 18k+ members, fragrance-focused)
- `r/Hyderabad` (local community)
- Basenotes Forums (global fragrance community, DA 62)
- Fragrantica Forums (DA 74)

**How to build links without spamming:**
- Spend 2–3 weeks contributing genuinely: answer questions about oud, attar, how to layer scents
- Only mention ZAHIDAAN when directly relevant ("I work for a small attar shop in Hyderabad — we stock...")
- On Basenotes: Create a brand profile (free). This gives 1 dofollow link from DA 62.
- On Fragrantica: Add ZAHIDAAN products to the database (free). Each product page becomes a citation.

**Do not:** Post "Check out our shop!" links. These get removed and earn bans, not links.

#### Tactic 4 — Guest Posts on Indian Lifestyle/Gifting Sites

Sites that accept guest posts in the gifting, lifestyle, or beauty space:

```
SheSays (shesays.co.in) — accepts beauty/lifestyle contributors
WeddingWire India blog — perfume/gifting content fits wedding audience
PopXO.com — Indian women's lifestyle, takes pitches
LifestyleAsia India (lifestyleasia.com/in) — luxury/lifestyle guest posts
```

**Pitch angle:** "Why Attar Makes the Perfect Eid Gift" / "5 Reasons Alcohol-Free Perfume is Trending in India" — these are editorial, not promotional, and can naturally link to ZAHIDAAN's shop or blog.

1 accepted guest post = 1 high-DA dofollow link + referral traffic.

#### Tactic 5 — Supplier / Manufacturer Mentions

If ZAHIDAAN sources from a specific oud supplier or attar distillery:
- Ask the supplier to add ZAHIDAAN to their "stockists" or "retail partners" page
- This earns a relevant, contextual link — often from a DA 30–50 domain

#### Tactic 6 — Hyderabad Business Directories

Beyond the national directories in Agent 13:

```

Telugu360 Business    → telugu360.com
```

#### Tactic 7 — Press Release on Launch

On launch day, publish a short press release (200–300 words) announcing ZAHIDAAN's launch and pan-India shipping. Distribute free via:

```

OpenPR.com (free, indexed, DA 55)

```

The press release should contain:
- The phrase "Zahidaan Attars & Perfumes, based in Patancheru, Hyderabad"
- A link to `zahidaan.in`
- A quote attributed to the founder

This earns 2–3 links immediately and sets a baseline entity signal for GEO.

## SECTION 10: E-E-A-T DEEP BUILD

Agent 13 mentions E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authority, Trust) as a factor for AEO. This section operationalises it. Google's quality raters actively look for E-E-A-T signals on YMYL-adjacent product sites (health, beauty, wellness), which includes perfume brands claiming natural/skin-friendly attributes.

### 10.1 Author Entity

The "ZAHIDAAN Fragrance Team" byline in Agent 13 is a placeholder. For real E-E-A-T, every blog post and FAQ should have:

**An author bio page at `/authors/zahid`:**
```
Name: Zahid
Role: Founder & Master Perfumer, ZAHIDAAN Attars & Perfumes
Experience: [X] years in fragrance · Specialisation in Arabian ouds and traditional Indian attars
Location: Patancheru, Hyderabad, Telangana
```

Include:
- A real photo (helps Google's author entity recognition)
- A link to the founder's personal Instagram or LinkedIn (connects the entity)
- A line about their personal philosophy of fragrance

All blog posts → add: `<link rel="author" href="https://zahidaan.in/authors/zahid"/>` in `<head>`.

### 10.2 About Page E-E-A-T Signals

The About page (`/about`) must include:

**Physical proof of business:**
- Embedded Google Map iframe pointing to the store
- Photo of the physical store frontage
- Photo of the founder in the store (confirms "Experienced" signal)

**Credentials and process:**
- How ZAHIDAAN sources its raw materials (oud wood? sandalwood? where from?)
- How attars are tested before stocking
- A line about the founder's journey into fragrance

**Press or mentions (as they accumulate):**
- A "As mentioned in:" section — even one JustDial feature or blogger review counts early on
- Links to all external citations

### 10.3 Product Page Trust Signals

Each product page must include (in addition to the schema already specified):

- **Ingredient transparency:** List the key fragrance components (saffron, oud, musk, etc.). Customers asking "is this alcohol-free?" need this visible.
- **Longevity claim with basis:** "8–10 hours longevity — tested on pulse points at 28°C" is more credible than just "long-lasting"
- **Concentration disclosure:** "Concentration: 100% pure oil" or "EDP: 20% concentration in carrier"
- **Allergen note (optional but trust-building):** "Free of alcohol, parabens, synthetic fixatives"

### 10.4 Review Schema — Activation Plan

The Product schema in Agent 13 uses a placeholder `reviewCount: 12`. This placeholder must be replaced with real data **before launch.**

**Pre-launch: Get 5 seed reviews.**
- Give 5 samples to friends/family/existing customers
- Ask them to leave a Google review AND message you their feedback (save these as text)
- Add their feedback as product reviews on the website (using Sanity or a review tool)
- These activate the `aggregateRating` schema

Once the schema shows real data, Google may display star ratings directly in search results (rich snippets) — a significant click-through-rate boost.
